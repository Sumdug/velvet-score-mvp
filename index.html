Velvet Score is a personal rating index. Scores are held locally in this browser and are never published.

The catalogue is restricted to adult public figures. “Perceived personality” records an impression of a person’s public persona, not a claim about their private character.

This prototype pulls a publicly available editorial image from Wikimedia Commons where possible. Proper catalogue curation, rights review and image licensing belong in the next phase.
